Data Deletion Instructions

Pilgrim Agency · "Claude connection" app

The app does not use Facebook Login and does not store personal data of Facebook users. If you believe the app holds any data related to you, or you want it removed:

  1. Send an email to petatore1@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. Include your name and the Facebook Page, ad account or user ID concerned.
  3. We will delete the data and confirm by email within 30 days.

Business owners can also revoke the app's access at any time in Meta Business Manager → Settings → Apps / Partners.
