About this app

"Claude connection" is an internal business tool operated by Pilgrim Agency. It uses the Meta Marketing API with a system user token to create and manage advertising campaigns, and to read advertising performance data, for ad accounts and Pages that Pilgrim Agency owns or has been granted access to by its clients.

What data we process

The app does not offer Facebook Login and does not collect personal data from members of the public. It processes only business data that is available to Pilgrim Agency in Meta Business Manager: ad account settings, campaigns, ad sets, ads, creatives, Page information and aggregated, non-personal performance metrics (impressions, clicks, spend, etc.).

How we use it

Data is used solely to set up, run, optimise and report on advertising campaigns for Pilgrim Agency and its clients. We do not sell data, do not use it to build user profiles and do not share it with third parties except service providers needed to operate the tool (hosting and AI assistant providers), who process it on our behalf.

Storage and security

The app does not run its own database. Access tokens are stored as encrypted environment variables on our hosting provider and are never published. Access can be revoked at any time in Meta Business Manager.
